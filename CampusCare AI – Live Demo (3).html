About CampusCare AI

CampusCare AI is a student support tool for UPM. It reads everyday campus signals, finds who may be struggling, explains why in simple words, and makes sure help arrives early. It is built for UPM InnoHub, Track 1: Predictive Model.

See your own status, get guides, reminders and a private chatbot.

Part 1

Why students leave their studies

Four problems cause most drop-outs. Often one problem causes the next.

📚

Academic barrier

Lessons feel too hard, so the student loses hope.

💸

Financial stress

Fees and living costs push students into long part-time jobs.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family pressure

Parents expect a high CGPA. This leads to burnout.

🧭

Loss of interest

The student feels the course is wrong and thinks about another career.

Part-time job→Missed classes→Bad exam results→Drop-out risk
Part 2

What the AI watches

Eight normal campus signals. No private messages are read. Students are told about this from the first day.

⏰

Attendance Time Analytics

Compares the usual arrival time with now. Shows tiredness or being late again and again.

🎬

PutraBLAST LMS Activity

How long lecture videos are watched, and whether notes are downloaded.

💬

Anonymous Chatbot NLP

Understands feelings from the words students write. No name is saved.

💳

Financial Portal Interaction

How often scholarship offers and financial aid pages are checked.

📝

Assessment Submission Velocity

Handing in work in the last 2 minutes or after midnight shows time pressure.

🤝

Peer-Group & Social Engagement

Was the student active in clubs before, and is now pulling away?

📚

Library Infrastructure Logs

Library visits, time spent there and e-book downloads.

✉️

UPM Email Integrated Logs

University mail traffic and job alerts, for example from JobStreet.

🎓 One email gives the full picture

All tracking uses the UPM email. When students also connect career sites like LinkedIn with it, the AI understands job search and money problems much better.

🔒 Your data, your choice

Do not want something tracked? Use a personal email for social media, YouTube and private things. At the start, only the student sees their own data and graphs. The university and others do not.

Part 3

What happens at each score

The AI turns all signals into one score from 0 to 100. A higher score means more risk.

Green
Score 0 to 40
The student is doing well. The AI sends a Congratulations message and keeps watching quietly.
Yellow
Score 41 to 70
The AI helps first. It finds the main problem and gives a step-by-step guide. If nothing improves in one week, the advisor gets an email.
Red
Score 71 to 100
A real person helps. The personal advisor gets an email and meets the student. Above 80, the UPM Counseling team also gets a red flag.
⏰ Smart reminders remind students early, before a small problem becomes a big one.
